Questions, answered

Is Aunt Harley connected to the internet?

No. After the one-time setup of the local model server, everything runs on-device. No cloud services, no accounts, no data leaves the machine.

Is she safe for kids?

Yes. Her whole persona is designed for a young audience — sweet, patient, encouraging, clean. She won't discuss adult topics, and she never asks for personal info.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10/11 PC with at least 8GB RAM and ~6GB free disk for the local model. Less RAM works with a smaller model.

Why is she named Harley?

She's part of the family. A favorite aunt with a bit of sparkle — just the age-appropriate, kid-sweet edition.
